# Content migration

Updated for the content-preserving rebuild. The initial block matrix was created before page changes. See CONTENT-PRESERVATION-MATRIX.md for blocks and CONTENT-COVERAGE-REPORT.md for the original / previous V2 / new V2 comparison.

Published prices are now preserved with date, tax and equipment conditions. The 2023 rental and 2024 IP promotions are historical references. Unverified claims are qualified, not used as universal promises. Original articles remain available through the 415-entry searchable library and the legacy routing policy; they are not declared obsolete. Twelve broad article-to-guide/industry consolidations from previous V2 have been reversed.

Core commercial pages have Thai and English versions. The 150 restored FAQ entries remain in Thai inside both locale shells, explicitly labelled on English pages. No invented customer quotations, ratings or credentials are included.
