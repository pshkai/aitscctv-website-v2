The text wordmark and small favicon preserve black/red AITS lettering without inventing a certification mark. No customer logos or endorsement claims are fabricated. Image layout preserves useful installation details; the project images have informative alternatives and explicit dimensions.

Noto Sans Thai is from the Google Fonts `ofl/notosansthai` repository, supplied under SIL Open Font License 1.1. `public/FONT-LICENSE.txt` accompanies the locally optimised font. The TTF source is retained; the browser uses only WOFF2. Latin text uses Arial/system sans-serif. No remote font service is contacted.

## Restored original assets

The header and footer now use the actual AITS logo from `https://aitscctv.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/cropped-cropped-Logo-AITS-1.png`, trimmed/resized as `public/brand/aits.webp`. The red design token is `#c53130`, matching the original-site accent recorded in the audit.

Seven customer logos were retrieved from the original `/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/` collection. Their source filenames (including misleading `ptt` names) are retained in `public/customers/`; rendered 360px-wide WebP copies are used on Home and Customers. Recognisable logos include Bangkokthonburi University, its demonstration school and Seven Stars Pharmaceutical. Unknown exact institutional identities are not inferred from filenames. Logos are identified as source customer evidence, not customer quotations or ratings.

`public/downloads/standards.pdf` preserves the original 2024 Let AITS Set Standard document. `unv-th.pdf` and `unv-en.pdf` preserve the UNV manuals linked from the original manual page. All downloads were verified to return PDF responses locally. The original fieldwork photographs remain in the hero, three project cards and detailed cases; no stock or generated project evidence was substituted.
