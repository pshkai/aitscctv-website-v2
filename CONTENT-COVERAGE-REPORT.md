# Content coverage report — content-preserving V2 rebuild

## Scope and evidence

The revised build has **63 topics / 126 locale routes**, compared with 29 / 58 in the previous V2. The homepage has nine major sections, six solution families, a separate alarm-system destination, three visible package prices, five FAQs and concise links to deeper pages.

`CONTENT-PRESERVATION-MATRIX.md` was started before page changes and then expanded as discovery completed. It contains **26,739 extracted text, image, download and exception records**. This is an inventory count, not 26,739 independently verified claims: repeated source sections and related-article material are consolidated. The JSON evidence is `discovery-v3/blocks.json`.

Discovery attempted **454 distinct public URLs** from the original page/post sitemaps. **299 bodies were retrieved; 155 were unavailable** (154 filesystem-read errors, including Windows-security-blocked HTML, and one other retrieval failure). Security settings were not disabled. Cached core-service evidence from the original audit supplemented discovery. The unavailable bodies are explicitly recorded and their URLs retained. Therefore this report does **not** claim a complete review of every historical article or prove that no testimonial exists anywhere in inaccessible material.

The library exposes **all 415 unique post URLs** in the original sitemap, including those whose bodies could not be inspected. Available page titles are used; missing titles are labelled from the URL slug. Original article bodies and their image collections remain on the existing public site. This is access preservation, not a claim that 415 articles have been rewritten locally. The future hosting plan must retain their original routes. Twelve previous broad article consolidations were reversed to avoid losing full articles behind short guides.

## FAQ preservation and editorial treatment

CCTV 32; access/attendance 24; networking 42; security 13; automation 10; meeting rooms 8; building services 21. Identical questions on alias/home pages are consolidated. Original source links remain attached. Source errors such as equating fibre mode with the number of cable cores, universal Wi-Fi coverage, guaranteed solar payback and instructions to silence fire detection are rewritten. Legal/permission questions are retained without categorical legal claims.

Commercial service pages and navigation have English counterparts. The restored **150-question corpus remains Thai**, visibly labelled and marked `lang="th"` on English pages. English FAQs are not falsely presented as fully translated. This preserves Thai depth without silently removing material while translation is pending.

## Boundaries that remain visible

No live deployment, WordPress changes, CRM/email connection or real enquiry was performed. Forms remain explicit simulations. Original article/video links require the current site and the documented legacy routing plan at any launch. The 155 inaccessible source bodies remain a review gap; retaining their URLs avoids silently discarding them. Source-logo reuse does not establish a current endorsement. No independently attributed testimonial was verified in the reviewed evidence.
